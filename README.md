# 📈 Meta Ad Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes advertising performance across Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram. The dashboard provides a complete view of campaign performance, audience engagement, conversions, budget utilization, and marketing effectiveness.

The objective is to help marketing teams optimize advertising strategies, improve conversion rates, and maximize return on investment (ROI).

---

## Business Problem

Marketing teams run multiple campaigns across different platforms, ad formats, audiences, and geographies. Without a centralized dashboard, it becomes difficult to evaluate campaign effectiveness and identify optimization opportunities.

This dashboard solves that problem by providing actionable insights into campaign performance, audience behavior, engagement patterns, and conversion efficiency.

## Dataset Overview

The project uses four datasets:

### ad_events

Stores user interactions such as impressions, clicks, shares, comments, and purchases.

### ads

Contains ad-level details including platform, ad type, target audience, and targeting criteria.

### campaigns

Stores campaign budgets, duration, and campaign information.

### users

Contains demographic information such as gender, age group, country, and interests.

## Key Insights

* CTR of 11.76% indicates strong ad engagement.
* Engagement Rate of 13.56% shows strong audience interest.
* Conversion Rate of 5.21% demonstrates moderate funnel effectiveness.
* Purchase Rate of 0.61% highlights opportunities for conversion optimization.
* Females aged 18–30 generate the highest engagement.
* Video and Story ads outperform Image and Carousel ads.
* India and Brazil generate significant engagement volume.
* User activity peaks during afternoon and evening hours.

---

## Business Recommendations

* Increase investment in Video and Story ad formats.
* Focus targeting on females aged 18–30.
* Improve landing page experience to increase purchases.
* Schedule campaigns during afternoon and evening hours.
* Optimize budget allocation toward high-performing geographies.
* Implement retargeting strategies to improve conversion rates.
